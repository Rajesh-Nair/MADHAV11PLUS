# 11+ Vocabulary & Jumbled-Word Resources

## Knowledge

- [11+ Jumbled Words — 11PlusEHelp](https://www.11plusehelp.co.uk/11-plus-english/11-plus-vocabulary/11-plus-jumbled-words)
  Sample 11+ jumbled-word questions in the exact exam format (word jumbled inside a
  sentence). Use for: extra practice sentences once a lesson's core word set is
  exhausted.
- [11+ Anagrams — Education Quizzes (Verbal Reasoning)](https://www.educationquizzes.com/11-plus/verbal-reasoning/anagrams-3/)
  Explains the anagram question type as it appears on real 11+ verbal reasoning
  papers, with worked examples. Use for: confirming question phrasing/format
  matches the real exam.
- [Twenty Amazing Anagram Puzzles — Twinkl](https://www.twinkl.com/blog/twenty-amazing-anagram-puzzles)
  Kid-friendly strategies: use the context clue, look for prefixes/suffixes,
  separate vowels from consonants, physically rearrange letters rather than stare
  at them. Use for: the letter-pattern-recognition step of the decoding strategy.
- [Greek and Latin Roots for 11+ Preparation — Eleven Plus Exams Tuition](https://www.elevenplusexamstuition.co.uk/greek-and-latin-roots-for-11-preparation/)
  11+-specific framing of why root knowledge helps decode unfamiliar words fast.
  Use for: lessons built around the Latin/Greek Roots sections of `indata/vocab_01.md`.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [The 11 Plus Exams Forum](https://www.elevenplusexams.co.uk/forum/11plus/) — UK's
  largest 11+ community (40,000+ members, regional sub-forums for verbal reasoning,
  English, SEN). Use for: parent-side troubleshooting, comparing what other
  children find hard, region-specific exam format questions.
  Note: this is a resource for the parent/adult running these sessions, not
  something to hand to a 10-year-old directly.

## Gaps

- No dedicated resource yet found specifically on homophones/homographs/homonyms
  at 11+ level — revisit when a lesson targets that vocab category.
- No resource yet vetted for the "jumbled word pair with missing letters" format —
  this will need to be designed largely from first principles (see `task02.md`).
