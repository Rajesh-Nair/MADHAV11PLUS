Jumbled Word Practice

This one page holds every jumbled-word batch, newest at the bottom. It keeps growing as new words are added — no new page needed each time. Vocabulary is tackled hardest-first rather than in list order, so the earliest batches are already some of the toughest words in the whole list.

Same rule as always: no peeking. Try C.L.U.E. first — Context, Letter chunks, Un-jumble by writing, Errand-check — and only open a clue if you're stuck. Each clue is tagged with the trick it's teaching. Full reminder: C.L.U.E. reference card.

This page can be long — work through any batch, in any order, across as many sittings as you like. Each batch below is collapsed by default; click its title to open it. Your progress is saved in this browser automatically, so closing the page and coming back later picks up right where you left off.
Questions about why an answer is right, or want to go over a word again? Just ask your teacher — that's what follow-up questions are for.
