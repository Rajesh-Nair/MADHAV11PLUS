Lesson 1 of the 11+ vocabulary course

Decode Your First Jumbled Word

Hi Madhav — on the 11+ paper, jumbled-word questions hide a real word inside a scrambled mess of letters, sitting inside a sentence. Right now, when you hit one of these, the plan is usually "try letters until something looks right." That works sometimes, but it's slow and it runs out of luck under a timer.

Today you're learning a strategy instead of a guess: C.L.U.E. Four steps, same order, every single time.

The C.L.U.E. strategy

C
Context first. Read the whole sentence before you touch the letters. What kind of word is missing?
L
Letter chunks. Look for pairs like TH, CH, QU, or endings like -ING, -TION, hiding in the jumble.
U
Un-jumble by writing. Write the letters down and move them around on paper — don't just stare at the scrambled order.
E
Errand-check. Put your answer back in the sentence. Does it actually make sense?

Full cheat sheet, printable: reference/jumbled-word-strategy.html

Watch it done: CAUTION

Cross the busy road with great care and NAUTICO, looking both ways twice.

C — Context: the sentence is about crossing a road safely. The missing word is probably a feeling or a way of behaving — like "carefully."

L — Letter chunks: the letters are N, A, U, T, I, C, O. Spot the C and try it at the front: "CAU..." — that's the start of several English words.

U — Un-jumble: writing out "CAU" + the leftover letters T, I, O, N gives "CAUTION."

E — Errand-check: "Cross the busy road with great care and CAUTION" — yes, that makes sense. Done.

Your turn

Use C.L.U.E. Type a guess whenever you want to check it — or ask for a clue first. No answer is shown until you've had a go.

Bonus round (optional): one more word

Recap

Next time a jumbled word shows up: Context, Letter chunks, Un-jumble by writing, Errand-check. Keep the reference card nearby until it's automatic.

Stuck, or want to know why an answer is right? Just ask — your teacher (that's the agent helping you through these lessons) is there for follow-up questions any time something doesn't click.
